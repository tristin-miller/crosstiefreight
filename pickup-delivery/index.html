Questions

      

      

        

          How fast can you pick up?
          
Book by noon and we can usually pick up the same day. Rush pickups depend on the schedule, so tell us your deadline in the form.

        

        

          How is the price set?
          
Each job gets a flat price based on distance, size and timing. We text it to you before anything is booked, so there are no surprise charges.

        

        

          Do you move furniture or do household moves?
          
No. We don't do household moves or move furniture. We pick up store purchases, boxes, tools, equipment and other items one person can safely load.

        

        

          Is there anything else you won't take?
          
We don't take hazardous materials like fuel, chemicals or paint. Anything heavy or oversized needs to be cleared with us first, so describe it in the form.

        

        

          What areas do you cover?
          
Austin and nearby areas. Put both ZIP codes in the form and we'll confirm.

        

        

          How do I pay?
          
Card, Venmo or Zelle once the job is done.
